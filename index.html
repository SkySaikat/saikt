05 / BEFORE WE BUILD

About the work, the process,

What does Saikat Chowdhury build?+

I build websites and web applications with Next.js, React, Node.js, PHP, and Laravel, along with custom WordPress plugins, e-commerce experiences, AI automation, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. My work connects the interface, business workflow, and underlying systems.

Where are you based, and do you work internationally?+

I am based in Chattogram (Chittagong), Bangladesh, in the GMT+6 time zone. I work remotely with clients and teams across Europe, North America, and Asia. I am also the founder of Wonder Web Studio and a web team lead.

Can you build a custom WordPress plugin?+

Yes. I build plugins for workflows that need more than an off-the-shelf tool. Examples include the Cyprus conference abstract submission and weighted-review plugin, a multi-site publishing integration, and SEO Duplicate Fixer for cleaning up WordPress metadata.

What can a custom MCP server do for my business?+

An MCP server connects a compatible AI assistant to your business tools through a defined set of actions. My Google Ads MCP supports reporting and preview-and-confirm campaign changes, while CallRail MCP connects calls, forms, attribution, and lead operations. The useful starting point is a specific workflow and clear access rules.

Can you migrate a WordPress website to Next.js?+

Yes. My migration work includes ITZ Digital and home-service websites. I map existing URLs, migrate content, and check redirects, metadata, structured data, and lead forms. That helps preserve useful content and discoverability, but no migration can guarantee unchanged search rankings.

How do we start a web development or automation project?+

Send a brief through the contact form or email hi@saikatchowdhury.com. Include what you want to build, your current website or tools, the problem you want to solve, and any target timeline or budget. I can then discuss the scope and a practical next step.
